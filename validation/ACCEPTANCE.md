# Evidence and acceptance rules

A phase is accepted only when its deliverables exist, claims have traceable evidence or an explicit limitation, calculations are reproducible, and a reviewer has recorded unresolved issues. Mark evidence as Observed, Inferred, Estimated, or Not Available. A failed or unavailable test is not a passing test. Never infer developer intent, original cost, or requirements without evidence. Human approval is required at the gates listed in `PLANS.md`.
