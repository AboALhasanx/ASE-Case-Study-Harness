# Evidence and acceptance rules

A phase is accepted when its deliverables exist, claims have traceable evidence or an explicit limitation, calculations are reproducible, and an independent critic or sequential self-review has recorded unresolved issues. Mark evidence as Observed, Inferred, Estimated, or Not Available. A failed or unavailable test is not a passing test. Never infer developer intent, original cost, or requirements without evidence. Phase gates are internal quality checks; they must not pause for human approval. Summarize limitations in the final output.
