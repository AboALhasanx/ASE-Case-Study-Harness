# Seminar outline (12–18 minutes; adjust to instructor request)

1. System, problem, and study question
2. Scope and exact source revision
3. Method and evidence rules
4. Lifecycle and reconstructed requirements
5. Planning and design findings
6. Metrics and quality/reliability evidence
7. Testing and maintenance findings
8. Limitations and what remains unknown
9. Conclusions tied to evidence
10. Individual contribution and viva handoff

Every chart and claim should link to the report or evidence ledger. Do not add new findings only to slides.
